The sweet bit, if you want one. Every breakfast here is savoury by design. If you want something sweet alongside, add it as a small finish rather than making the meal sweet: 80 g of berries (about 35 kcal), a small pear (80 kcal), or 10 g of 85% dark chocolate (55 kcal). None of them touch the protein and all of them fit inside the daily allowance. Oats appear nowhere on the list.

Portions are per person, per meal. The figures on each card are for one serving, so a day is simply its three meals added up. Dinners are cooked at twice the quantity shown, but you still eat one portion tonight and one tomorrow.

Leftovers. Cool them fast and get them in the fridge within an hour. Cooked fish is good for two days and everything here is eaten inside one.

Macros are estimates from standard food composition values and Mercadona pack labels; treat them as ±10%. Weigh the fish and the oil — those two move the number most. If the scale hasn't shifted in three weeks, drop 100 kcal rather than cutting a meal, since three meals is what makes this liveable for you.

Not medical advice. If you're on medication or have a thyroid, blood-sugar or kidney condition, run the protein target past your GP first.
